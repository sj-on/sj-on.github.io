writers are non-mythical beings, universally burdened with the task of preserving history.

i mean, take a look at the epics. all of that stuff happened for real ;) would you still have believed *it* happened if it weren't for a bunch of grey-hairdos (mostly male) who committed themselves to keeping logs of events as they kept happening? (why else would it keep passing down generations if it was farce?)

this is one of the *why's* i have to become a writer. i get paid in fame and attention coins, and i join tomorrow.
