welcome to the truth factory (ttf), where the front is pretty and the back is prettier. this is a collection of cards about things you didn’t ask for but are going to get.

the truth factory is a state-of-the-art facility dedicated to the industrial-scale production of truths: emotional, technical, and whatever other kinds they come in, and statements that sounded correct at the time of writing.

every truth is carefully assembled using only the finest ingredients:

- angst
- sleep deprivation
- internet access
- vibes
- and time dilation
