Governments collect taxes and spend on public goods: infrastructure, schools, health, defense, welfare, and interest payments. Your taxes are pooled and redistributed.

Modern money is managed by central banks and banks via lending. Governments tax, borrow, and create liquidity — it's a mix of policy and economic activity.
