sister channels — the back

sister channels are weird in the sense that a sibling (the one with the "main character syndrome") gives birth to them. this explains why there are constant callbacks directed towards the younger baby (with a familiar bossing around in the background which is a characteristic feature of this relationship).

a sister channel is a classic example of a main channel gone feral. think "when parents want a chartered accountant in the family but you want to do a longform analysis of songs composed in natural minor that would've sounded better in dorian mode," and you'll know why it's always the damn sisters!

you may or may not question the authority, but you can always question your sanity.

[answers]

  1. because there's not enough chaos in the world to begin with.

  2. a total collapse of the sibling ego. the "bossing around" turns into a desperate, existential identity crisis. when the "dorian mode" deep dive gets more hits than the "professional tech review," the creator realizes the feral child was the one they should have been all along.

  3. the sister channel is the only place where the creator is truly honest, which is exactly why the main channel (and the audience) is so terrified of it. one is a career; the other is a confession.

  4. deep within our hearts, we all want to believe we are doing okay, but the brain knows. it always does.
