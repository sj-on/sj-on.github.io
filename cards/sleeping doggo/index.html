sleeping doggo — the back

a sleeping doggo is a sleeping beauty who cannot be awoken by true love's first kiss because dogs can only bite.

when dreaming, a doggo may twitch or whimper. this is a normal behaviour but may sometimes indicate discomfort or a nightmare. no one ever signs up for a nightmare (especially not a sleeping doggo!) and it is not easy to break out of one.

what can help them wake up, rather, is a soft pat on the head as an assurance that everything will be alright.
